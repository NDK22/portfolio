Intro

I am a Senior Software Engineer with 8+ years of experience across distributed systems, real-time streaming platforms, scalable data infrastructure, and analytics-driven software engineering. My current work focuses on PySpark, Databricks, Delta Lake, SQL, and production-grade data workflows that support telemetry-based inference and alerting systems.

At KPIT Technologies, I support Cummins digital solutions by building and enhancing streaming inference architecture, alert suppression workflows, alert quality analytics, and framework stabilization capabilities for mining-market predictive models. This work combines data engineering, model execution, and operational analytics in a real-time environment.

Before KPIT, I worked at Accenture on enterprise retail data platforms for Best Buy Canada and WHSmith, covering ETL automation, Oracle and AWS-based data integration, forecasting support, production operations, reporting systems, and CI/CD-oriented deployment workflows.

I also completed applied research at the University of Texas at Arlington and UTARI, where I worked on computer vision, motion analysis, ETL automation, and machine learning systems for cognitive assessment and AI-powered golf swing analysis.

I hold a Master of Science in Data Science from the University of Texas at Arlington and a Bachelor of Technology in Mechanical Engineering from VIT. My work is grounded in practical engineering delivery, data-driven problem solving, and continuous technical growth.
